iPhone Secure install

Installs over-the-air on registered devices. Open this page on your iPhone and tap install.

⬇ Install for iPhone
Version 1.0.0 · iOS 15+
  1. Open this page on the iPhone (Safari) and tap Install for iPhone.
  2. Tap Install when iOS asks — ATRION appears on your Home Screen.
  3. First launch: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap ATRION → Trust.
  4. Open ATRION and sign in.

Device not registered? Ad-hoc installs only work on devices we've added. Send your UDID (Safari → get.udid.io) and we'll enable it.
