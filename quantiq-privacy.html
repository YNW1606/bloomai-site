Quantiq — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Quantiq (“Quantiq”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) is a professional market-analysis terminal for the Indian markets (NSE). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. Quantiq is an analysis-only tool: it does not place trades and does not provide investment advice.

In short: we collect the email address you sign in with and basic session information needed to secure your account and run the service. We do not sell your data and do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information we collect

  • Account email address — used to authenticate and identify your account. Accounts are provisioned by an administrator; there is no public self sign-up.
  • Session & usage information — such as login times, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and device name. This is used only to secure your account, prevent unauthorized access, and operate the service.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise GPS location, health data, or financial account credentials. Quantiq does not connect to your brokerage to place orders.

2. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to the app’s features.
  • To keep your account secure and maintain the integrity of the service.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

We use your information only for these app-functionality and security purposes. We do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Market data

Quantiq displays market data (including quotes, option chains, and derived analytics) sourced from exchanges and third-party data providers. Such data may be delayed and is provided for informational and analytical purposes only. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

4. Data storage & security

Your session token and profile are stored on your device using the operating system’s encrypted secure storage (iOS Keychain). Data transmitted between the app and our servers is protected in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. The app also includes screen-capture protection to help safeguard on-screen information.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the app (for example, cloud hosting), and only as needed to provide the service, or where required by law.

6. Data retention

We retain account and session information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact).

7. Your choices & rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the address below. Because access is administrator-provisioned, account changes can also be made by your administrator.

8. Children

Quantiq is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this policy or your data: ai@bloomai.technology.
